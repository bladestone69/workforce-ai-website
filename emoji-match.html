The project

Quick to understand. Built to keep changing.

Emoji Match uses a familiar visual language and accessible mechanic, then layers in different grids, level goals, obstacles, and boosters.

01 / LEVELS

Long-form progression

More than 200 levels create a structured journey rather than a single repeated board.

02 / VARIETY

Changing grids

Board sizes range from compact arrangements to more complex spatial challenges.

03 / PRESSURE

Obstacles and goals

Blocks, growing obstacles, timed bombs, and boosters change the decisions a player makes.

04 / IDENTITY

Bright visual language

Recognisable emoji keep the game readable while supporting a playful, colourful tone.

Design principle
Use something instantly familiar, then create depth through the rules around it.
