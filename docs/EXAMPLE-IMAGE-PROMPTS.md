# Example website image prompts

These original assets were created with the built-in image generation tool, then saved as optimized JPEGs in `examples/images/`. They are concept imagery, not photographs of real clients or businesses.

## Lodge — `examples/images/lodge.jpg`

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original website hero and gallery photograph for a fictional South African small lodge. Scene: a modest but inviting riverside guest lodge in the South African countryside, timber deck overlooking a narrow calm river and indigenous greenery, late afternoon. Subject: real guesthouse exterior and setting, not a resort fantasy. Composition: wide landscape editorial photograph with clear architectural detail and natural negative space. Lighting: warm natural sunlight, believable shadows. Textures: weathered wood, stone, water reflections, imperfect real-world details. Constraints: no people, no text, no logo, no watermark, no resemblance to a specific existing lodge or website. This same photo will appear on both before and after website concepts.

## Repairs — `examples/images/repairs.jpg`

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original website photograph for a fictional South African local property repair business. Scene: a working tradesperson in practical work clothes repairing a leaking outdoor tap or pipe at a modest suburban Johannesburg home, toolbox nearby, believable home exterior. Subject: competent local craftsperson focused on a small repair, candid not posed, no uniforms or logos. Composition: wide editorial photograph, enough context to understand the service, usable crop on desktop and mobile. Lighting: natural daylight, realistic skin and material texture, lived-in home details. Constraints: no words, no logos, no watermark, no resemblance to a specific company or individual. The same photograph is for the before and after versions of one fictional company.

## Advisory — `examples/images/advisory.jpg`

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original website hero photograph for a fictional South African small-business advisory firm. Scene: two small-business owners in a modest bright Johannesburg office reviewing paper documents and a laptop with an advisor across the table, practical discussion, not a corporate boardroom. Subject: friendly, credible professional collaboration with diverse South African adults, candid gestures, natural expressions, realistic clothing and skin texture. Composition: wide editorial photograph with room for responsive crop; no readable screen text. Lighting: soft daylight, restrained warm-neutral palette. Constraints: no words, logos, watermark, fake charts, awards, or resemblance to a specific business or individual. This same photograph appears in before and after concepts for one fictional company.
