# Websites and Apps redesign

## Purpose
Keep the studio offer clear: Websites help a business explain its offer and generate enquiries; Apps help people complete a task or workflow. They share a process but not a sales page.

## Routes
- `websites-apps.html` remains the Websites route so existing links do not break. Remove the unsuitable hero image and all app scope from this page.
- `apps.html` is the dedicated Apps route, with discovery-led pricing rather than a misleading fixed package.
- `examples/lodge.html`, `examples/trades.html`, and `examples/practice.html` are self-contained illustrative before/after website concepts. They are not presented as client work or measured results.
- Home and Services offer separate Website and App paths. Contact form options and links must match those paths.

## Website examples
Each example has an accessible Before/After toggle, working in-page navigation, and an enquiry link back to the studio. The same original generated photograph is used in each Before/After pair. No real client's identity, exact layout, testimonials, credentials, rates or measured results are recreated or invented.

- Lodge: an older beige accommodation template with a zooming hero, floating welcome box and moving marquee becomes a quiet riverside editorial site. This is the intentionally over-animated example; reduced-motion settings disable it.
- Repairs: a crowded blue-and-orange service-business brochure becomes a high-contrast utility site with work types, coverage guidance and a quote route.
- Advisory: a dated corporate-blue site built on jargon becomes a restrained ivory-and-ink practice page focused on audience, decisions and a first call.

The examples are fictional, not live booking, repair or advisory businesses. Buttons lead to relevant sections or to Lockdown Studios; no demo pretends to submit to the fictional business.

Industry pattern references (research only, not copied): accommodation sites commonly surface rooms, amenities and booking enquiry; local trade sites surface work types, area and quote/callout routes; advisory firms surface service scope and a consultation route. Examples reviewed: Kosmos Lodge, MultiMan, and Petrichor Consulting.

## Visual rules
Carry the Home page's dark star field, translucent section bands, compact cards, typography, teal action colour, and restrained service accents into the service pages. Keep pricing and CTA blocks legible against the stars. Respect reduced motion and mobile widths.

## Release checks
Verify all new links and assets resolve locally; keyboard-operate the toggles and anchor links; check mobile layout, contrast, and contact preselection; review copy and prices before deployment. No production deployment is part of this local change.
